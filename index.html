How It Works

1

Connect to Wi-Fi

Make sure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network (or PC is connected to your phone's hotspot).

2

Open QuickDrop

Launch QuickDrop on your phone and open QuickDrop on your PC. Both devices will appear on each other's radar.

3

Share Instantly

Tap a device or drag-and-drop files to transfer at up to 100 MB/s. Photos and videos appear directly in your Gallery!
